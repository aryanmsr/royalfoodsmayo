About Us

Royal Taste Foods Ltd. is a proudly Zambian food manufacturer committed to crafting exceptional condiments. From humble beginnings in Lusaka, we've grown into a trusted household name by focusing on quality, consistency and innovation.

Our signature Prime and Mama mayonnaise ranges are made with carefully selected ingredients and time-honoured recipes, ensuring a rich and smooth taste in every jar. We believe good food brings people together and are dedicated to enhancing your meals with products you can rely on.
